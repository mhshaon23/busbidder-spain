# Reference check

Checked on 30 October 2026. Every in-text citation in the report was compared with its source. Six DOIs were confirmed on [Crossref](https://api.crossref.org). The sources were read in full where a legal open-access copy exists; otherwise the publisher page, a library catalogue or secondary literature was used.

| Reference | How it was verified | Claim in the report | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Belton & Gear (1983), *Omega* 11(3), 228–230 | Crossref (DOI, pages); [Wikipedia: Rank reversals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rank_reversals_in_decision-making) | First to discuss rank reversal in AHP (Section 7.5) | Supported |
| Friedman & Nissenbaum (1996), *ACM TOIS* 14(3), 330–347 | Crossref; full text read ([author copy](https://nissenbaum.tech.cornell.edu/papers/biasincomputers.pdf)) | Kinds of bias (Section 8.5) | **Corrected.** The report said they distinguish two kinds of bias; the paper defines three (preexisting, technical, emergent). The paragraph now names all three and uses the paper's own example of biased flight ranking in airline reservation systems. |
| Gamma, Helm, Johnson & Vlissides, *Design patterns* | Open Library; [Wikipedia: Design Patterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_Patterns) | "Favor object composition over class inheritance" (Section 3.4) | Supported. **Year corrected** from 1994 to 1995: the book was released in October 1994, but its copyright year is 1995, which APA 7 uses. |
| Hwang & Yoon (1981), Springer | Crossref; [Springer](https://link.springer.com/book/9783540105589); [table of contents](https://ebooks.mpdl.mpg.de/ebooks/Record/EB000662007/TOC) | The weighted sum model is an established MCDM method (Section 1.1) | **Softened.** The report said the weighted sum model is "the most widely used" method; no wording in the sources supporting this was found. It now says "one of the simplest and best-established methods". |
| Jahan & Edwards (2015), *Materials & Design* 65, 335–342 | Crossref; summaries of the paper in later literature | The choice of normalisation influences rankings (Section 8.1) | Supported |
| Meyer (1988), *Object-oriented software construction* | [Wikipedia: Open–closed principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open%E2%80%93closed_principle) | Origin of the open–closed principle (Section 3.5) | Supported |
| Miller (2019), *Artificial Intelligence* 267, 1–38 | Crossref; full text read ([arXiv 1706.07269](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.07269)) | Contrastive explanations (Section 5.6) | **Corrected.** The report said people find contrastive explanations "more useful than complete ones", which the paper does not say. It now states the paper's two findings separately: explanations are contrastive (why P instead of Q) and selected (one or two causes rather than a complete cause). |
| Python Software Foundation, *Descriptor guide* | [docs.python.org](https://docs.python.org/3/howto/descriptor.html) | Definition of a descriptor (Section 3.5) | Supported. Wording aligned with the guide ("customise attribute lookup, storage, and deletion"). |
| Ramalho (2022), *Fluent Python* (2nd ed.) | Open Library; [O'Reilly chapter 23](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/fluent-python-2nd/9781492056348/ch23.html) "Attribute Descriptors" | Definition of a descriptor (Section 3.5) | Supported |
| Saaty (1980), *The analytic hierarchy process*, McGraw-Hill | Open Library; [Wikipedia: AHP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytic_hierarchy_process) | AHP uses pairwise comparisons that force trade-offs (Section 8.6) | Supported |
| Triantaphyllou (2000), *Multi-criteria decision making methods* | Crossref (Springer DOI); [author's book page](https://www.csc.lsu.edu/trianta/Books/DecisionMaking1/backup/Book_DM1.htm) | The weighted sum model is an established MCDM method (Section 1.1) | **Softened** together with Hwang & Yoon (see above). Publisher given as Springer, matching the DOI. |

No invented sources were found. All 11 references exist with the details given. Three citing sentences misstated their source and were corrected, one claim was softened, and one year was corrected.

**Note:** Friedman & Nissenbaum and Miller were read in full. For the books, the cited points are well-known, standard attributions, but the book text itself was not available. If you have library access, checking Gamma et al. (p. 20) and the introduction of Triantaphyllou (2000) directly is still worthwhile.
